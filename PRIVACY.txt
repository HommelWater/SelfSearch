Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026
Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how SelfSearch ("we", "our", or "the extension") collects, uses, and handles user data. SelfSearch is a client-only search engine: it captures pages you visit, extracts keywords, stores them locally on your device, and lets you search them — including across a peer-to-peer network you control.
Information We Collect

Indexing: When you click "Index this page", the extension reads the current page's text content and (optionally) captures a screenshot. Keywords, a title, and a description are extracted locally on your device from the page's own text. You may also add your own keywords. The extracted metadata, together with the page URL and timestamp, is stored locally on your device in the extension's IndexedDB. The screenshot, if captured, is also stored locally on your device.

We do not operate any servers and do not collect, store, or process your data. All data stays on your device unless you explicitly share it as described below.
How Your Data Is Used

By the extension: The extracted metadata is used solely to build your local, searchable index. No third-party services are contacted for indexing. If you enable the optional local vision model, data only travels to a process on your own machine.
Peer-to-peer sharing

The extension can connect to other users you explicitly trust and search across their indexes. When enabled, the following is shared with peers you have added:

    Search queries you enter.
    Text metadata (URL, title, description, keywords, timestamp) of pages in your index.
    Your profile (name, avatar, bio) if you set one.

Screenshots are never shared with peers. Peer-to-peer sharing is opt-in and only involves peers you explicitly add. Your profile and trust declarations are published to public relay servers so peers can find and recognize you.
Information Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties. The only transfers occur when you explicitly enable peer-to-peer search: text metadata and queries are exchanged with peers you have added.
Data Security

Data is stored locally on your device. Peer-to-peer connections use WebRTC data channels with end-to-end signatures.
User Control

    Indexing only occurs when you actively click the extension icon.
    You can delete any indexed page from the search page.
    Peer-to-peer sharing is opt-in and controlled per peer.
    You can uninstall the extension to remove all locally stored data.

Data Retention

We (the extension developer) do not retain any user data. All data is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension or delete it via the search page.
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the extension store listings.
